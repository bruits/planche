# Editing

Press ⌘K, or Ctrl+K outside macOS, to find any command by name. The list gives each command's keys and says why one cannot run right now. A right-click opens the commands that apply to the selection, or to the board when nothing is under the pointer. The line above the toolbar says what the current tool or gesture does and which keys change it. Every key is in [shortcuts](./shortcuts.md).

## Tools

The toolbar groups its tools. Select, Hand, and Add images come first, then the pen, the highlighter, and the eraser, then arrows, lines, and shapes, and last text, sticky notes, and comments.

- The eraser removes what a click would select. A drag removes all it passes over, except the image or note it starts on.
- The pen and the highlighter stay the tool after each stroke. Shift draws a straight line.
- Text starts where you click, or at the width you drag.
- A comment is a pin that keeps one size on screen whatever the zoom. Its text shows while you hover it.

## Selecting and groups

A click selects the outermost group under the pointer. Double-click a group, or use Go inside, to select its own elements. Esc steps back one thing at a time. It closes a card, then leaves the tool, then goes back up out of a group, then clears the selection.

## Locking

Lock what should stay put, such as the images a board is built on. Clicks and selection rectangles go through a locked element, and no edit reaches it until it is unlocked. It still moves with its group, or with what it sticks to, and an animated image or video keeps playing. To unlock one, rest the pointer on it with nothing selected and use Unlock, or right-click it and choose Unlock.

## Moving, scaling, and turning

Drag a selection with ⌥, or Alt outside macOS, to move a copy of it. Corners scale, the sides of a lone note, sticky note, or shape stretch it, and a drag from just outside a corner turns it. The arrow keys nudge by one pixel, ten with Shift, or one grid step while snapping to the grid.

With a grid shown, what you move or draw snaps to its lines. Snap to neighbours lines things up with the sides, middles, and gaps of what is beside them, and shows guides while it does. Hold ⌘, or Ctrl outside macOS, to move or draw freely. For a move, press it once the drag is under way, since ⌘ with a click adds to or takes from the selection.

## What sticks

The ends of an arrow or a line stick to the image, note, sticky note, or shape they land on, and follow it from then on. A note, sticky note, shape, stroke, or comment set down whole on an image, a note, a sticky note, or a shape that is filled or holds text sticks to it the same way. What sticks to an image keeps to the same spot of the picture through moves, scales, turns, crops, and flips. Hold ⌘, or Ctrl outside macOS, while drawing or moving to keep something from sticking.

## Style

Select something to show a chip that opens its style card. What you set there applies to everything selected, and becomes the style that tool draws with next, unless you hold ⌥ or Alt. Copy style and Paste style carry a style from one element to others. Palette colours follow the theme, so a board stays readable in light and dark. Colours of your own stay as picked.

The card follows the selection from one element to the next, and a click on nothing closes it unless Keep style open, in Settings, is on.

## Images

Image edits never touch the image's file. Crop, as a rectangle or an ellipse, rotate, flip, and greyscale can all be undone or reset later. Arrange packs images in rows by name, by size, by colour, or at random, and Same height or Same width sizes them alike. Open source opens the web page an image came from.

Animated images and videos have their own controls, see [media](./media.md).

## Copy, paste, and export

Elements copy and paste between boards and between windows. A paste brings their images along as long as the window they were copied from stays open, until the next copy. A lone image copied also goes to the clipboard as a PNG, for other apps.

Export as PNG frames the selection on the board's colour, on white in the light theme's colours, or on nothing. Its choices of margin and size last from one export to the next. Copy as PNG puts the same picture on the clipboard.
